Weekly Meal Plan

Here is an example of a weekly meal plan

Monday

  • Breakfast: Bowl of oatmeal with fruits and yogurt
  • Lunch: Grilled chicken salad
  • Dinner: Baked salmon with vegetable

Tuesday

  • Breakfast: Eggs white omelette with spinach
  • Lunch: Vegetable and Chicken soup
  • Dinner: Tuna, salad with avocado

Wednesday

  • Breakfast: Protein smoothie with banana and almonds
  • Lunch: Grilled chicken breast with brown rice
  • Dinner: Quinoa salad with vegetables

Thursday

  • Breakfast: Whole wheat bread with avocado and tomato
  • Lunch: Grilled salmon with mashed potatoes
  • Dinner: Chicken salad with walnuts

Friday

  • Breakfast: Greek yogurt with fruits and granola
  • Lunch: Chickpea and vegetable salad
  • Dinner: Beef steak with steamed brocoli
